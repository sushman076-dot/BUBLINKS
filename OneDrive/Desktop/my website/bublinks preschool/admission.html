PLAN AHEAD

Documents and age groups.

Here is a useful starting point for your admission enquiry.

Documents to Prepare

Please keep the following documents ready and confirm the final requirements with the school.

  • Child's Aadhaar card
  • Child's birth certificate
  • Parents' Aadhaar cards
  • Child's passport-size photographs

Please check with us before submitting original documents or additional paperwork.

Programs & Age Groups

These age ranges can help you identify a suitable program to discuss with us.

Playgroup 2–3 years
Nursery 3–4 years
Junior KG 4–5 years
Senior KG 5–6 years
Daycare 1.5–7 years

If your child is younger than the listed preschool age groups, please contact us to discuss suitability.
